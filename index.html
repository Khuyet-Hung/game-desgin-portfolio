Receive Clues→ Solve Puzzle→ Find Station→ Take Photo→ Complete Challenge→ Earn Points→ Find Next Station

Each team received a list of five clues. Solving a clue revealed information needed to locate a station. At the station, the team completed a challenge to earn points.

The original game material included station clues, hints, challenges, and rules for keeping the team together.

After 60% of the play time, a Secret Station became available. It offered a high-value scoring opportunity, giving lower-scoring teams a reason to keep competing instead of mentally giving up early.

CorePrediction

Turn a simple “which food has fewer calories?” question into a competitive prediction game where accuracy matters.

Core Interaction

Each round presents two food images. Teams identify which food has fewer calories and estimate the difference between the two.

The game rewards both correctness and estimation accuracy. A correct identification provides a baseline score, while the bonus favors the team whose estimate is closer to the actual calorie difference.

BONUS SCORE = ACTUAL CALORIE DIFFERENCE − PREDICTION ERROR

  • Double-score card
  • Information card revealing which food has more calories

Power-ups introduce an additional strategic layer. Players must decide when information or score multiplication is more valuable instead of relying only on knowledge.
